Refund policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

1. General policy

Duck Plus payments are generally non-refundable. A refund may be provided when you paid but did not receive the subscription or paid features, when a persistent technical defect prevents access and cannot be fixed within a reasonable time, or when a refund is required by applicable law.

2. Mandatory rights

This policy does not limit non-waivable consumer rights. Stripe securely processes Duck Plus payments, but Duck remains responsible for providing the purchased service and handling eligible refund requests.

3. Cancellations

You may cancel Duck Plus at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals and normally takes effect at the end of the current paid billing period; unused time is not normally refunded.

4. Requesting help

Open the affected server in Duck’s dashboard and select Cancel or manage subscription. For refund help, include the Stripe receipt email, affected Discord server, purchase date, and a description of what was not delivered.
