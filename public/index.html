Safety architecture

Duck can’t just ban people because an AI said so.

AI output is advisory. Consequential actions move through explicit approval and fresh Discord checks at the moment they run.

Read how review works →
  1. 1
    Local rules run firstRate and content protections do not need an AI verdict.
  2. 2
    A human reviews the proposalOnly authorized moderators receive action controls.
  3. 3
    Permissions are checked againMember access, bot permissions, and role hierarchy must still pass.
  4. 4
    The result is recordedEach server keeps its own readable moderation trail.
