03

Build an AutoMod ladder

AutoMod runs locally before optional AI processing. Enable the module, then choose the protections you actually want:

  • Rate guards — set one server-wide member delay and optional channel overrides. Staff with Manage Messages are exempt.
  • Content filters — locally filter severe language, sexual terms, Discord invites, excessive capitals, and mention spam.
  • Custom blocked words — Plus servers can add up to 100 server-specific words or phrases.
  • Escalation — choose violations per warning, warnings per removal, then kick or softban.
  • Experimental AI scanning — choose a rules channel, scan channels, and a private review destination. Duck caches recent rule text briefly, sends it with a message to a fixed free model, and posts a suggestion with Go To Message and Take Action.
Hierarchy and humans still win. AI never acts automatically. A moderator must choose an action, then Duck re-fetches the message and member and re-checks live permissions and both moderator and bot role hierarchy.
04

Create no-code custom actions

Custom Actions are an if-this-then-that builder—not a JavaScript executor. Each rule can match every message, text a message contains, or text it starts with. Narrow it to a channel or user ID, then choose an allowlisted result.

Welcome response

If a message starts with hello duck, reply with Hi {user}!

Channel reaction

If anyone speaks in #showcase, react with 💚.

Plus moderation

If a member posts a blocked trigger in one channel, warn, timeout, kick, or softban.

Templates

Replies support {user}, {username}, {channel}, and {server}.

Duck runs at most three matching actions per message, rate-limits repeated triggers, and never evaluates uploaded code.

05

Build community, then understand Plus

Community Studio controls safe join roles, spam-resistant Pond Levels, /rank, /leaderboard, /suggest, voting, staff decisions, and a jump-linked Starboard. Color Dock adds a server palette: members use its picker, /color, or /colors, and Duck replaces their old palette role automatically. Choose the compact starter palette or the 30-color spectrum template. The honeypot can publish a persistent counter embed from AutoMod.

Choose a server, then use View plan to open its dedicated Plus page. Each subscription and owner grant belongs to that one Discord server, never the whole user account. Your desktop profile menu links to a separate Account center for Discord identity, session security, and server access.

  • Free — 5 safe custom actions, 6-reply memory, one join role, Pond Levels, proposals, classic Starboard, 12 Color Dock colors, 10 reaction roles, and 5 ticket types.
  • Duck Plus — 25 actions, 16-reply memory, 30 Color Dock colors with random join assignment, 5 join roles, 10 level rewards, anonymous proposals, custom Starboard, 10 scheduled posts, 25 reaction roles, 10 ticket types with transcripts, premium AI, and ElevenLabs TTS.
  • Three paid months — 50 actions, 30-reply AI memory, and server-specific bot branding.
  • Six paid months — unlimited custom actions and memory of 50 recent Duck replies.
  • Owner Plus — every loyalty perk unlocks immediately for that server.

The Plan page shows the current tier, renewal or access-end date, billing controls, loyalty progress, current perks, and the next milestone. Conversation memory remains bounded, expires after six hours, and stays isolated by Discord server.

Commands and tools

Talk to Duck in its setup channel, mention @Duck, reply to one of its messages, use a slash command, or use the configured prefix.

Natural language

“Duck, timeout @member for 10 minutes for spam.”

Slash command

/slowmode seconds:15

Prefix command

!warn @member repeated spam

Approval modes

  • Ask for approval — the safest starting point; actions wait for confirmation.
  • Approve for me — trusted requests can proceed under the configured policy.
  • Agent mode — more autonomous, while requester permissions and hierarchy remain enforced.
Recommended: begin with Ask for approval, test commands in a private staff channel, and grant only the permissions your moderators need.
